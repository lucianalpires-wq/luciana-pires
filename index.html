FARM Rio · 2025—26 · Global Brand Partnerships · Strategic Project Manager

FARM Rio × Barbour

A Brazilian brand and a British heritage label, brought together in one collaboration — sold out within days, renewed for a second drop and launched across five continents.

partnership strategy · go-to-market · ESG
ContextFARM Rio is a renowned Brazilian fashion brand with a growing international presence, known for its vibrant prints, distinctive design and strong cultural identity. With over 100 stores, the brand operates across Brazil and international markets, including New York, Miami, Los Angeles and Paris.The collaboration between FARM Rio and Barbour brought British countryside heritage together with FARM Rio’s energy, colours and signature prints — and quickly became a case of connection between brands, cultures and audiences.
The challengeBringing two strong identities together without diluting either, and staying relevant in every market while honouring cultural diversity. Too often, brands engage with cultures — especially Brazil — at a surface level: referencing without depth, aestheticizing without context.
What I didLed the project end to end alongside marketing and branding — translating the partnership strategy into execution. Coordinated the integrated go-to-market across product, marketing, retail and creative teams; contributed to the collaboration narrative; worked with ESG to bring socio-environmental values into the project; and managed the day-to-day relationship with Barbour, agencies and internal stakeholders.

02
Olympikus — Vulcabras · 2021—24 · Brand Strategic Project Manager

Running, community & ESG

I activated running communities across Brazil, brought fashion brands and creators into the brand, and co-developed an initial ESG strategy for Olympikus and Vulcabras — through events, content and partnerships in the years Olympikus became Brazil’s most-worn running shoe.

community · brand events · ESG strategy
ContextOlympikus, the largest Brazilian sports brand with international presence, is crafted by 17,000 Brazilians and produces millions of pairs of footwear every year.At this scale, running is not just a category — it’s a cultural movement. In 2025 alone, Brazil added 2 million new runners, and Olympikus sits at the centre of this community through events, products and partnerships.
The challengeTurn running from a product category into a cultural platform — connecting brand, community, partners and purpose across Brazil, while keeping consistency and relevance at scale.
What I didActivated Olympikus’s running community across Brazil — from flagship events to regional activations, influencer partnerships and content that celebrated diverse runner profiles.Coordinated one of the brand’s largest running events end to end, including social initiatives with the local community — managing logistics, partner engagement and brand experience.Acted as the bridge between brand, partners and internal teams, aligning business goals with sustainability — including an initial ESG brand strategy, connecting Vulcabras’ ESG team with FARFARM, an external sustainability partner.
